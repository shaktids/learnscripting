The heading tag is used in HTML to define headings of a page. Headings are defined by &lt;hn> , with “n” being a number between 1 and 6, and determines which position a heading has in the hierarchy of the heading structure.

        There are six different HTML headings which are defined with the &lt;h1> to &lt;h6> tags, from highest level h1 (main heading) to the least level h6 (least important heading).&lt;h1> is the largest heading and &lt;h6> is the smallest heading.

Example:

## &lt;h1>Heading no 1&lt;h1>

## &lt;h2>Heading no 2&lt;h2>

&lt;h3>Heading no 3&lt;h3>

**&lt;h4>Heading no 4&lt;h4>**

**&lt;h5>Heading no 5&lt;h5>**

&lt;h6>Heading no 6&lt;h6>

See the example:

```
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>

<head>
    <title>Heading elements</title>
</head>

<body>
    <h1>This is heading no. 1 </h1>
    <p>This is the first heading of the page</p>
    <h2>This is Heading no. 2</h2>
    <p>This is the second heading of the page </p>
    <h3>This is heading no. 3</h3>
    <p>This is the third heading of the page</p>
    <h4>This is heading no. 4 </h1>
        <p>This is the fourth heading of the page</p>
        <h5>This is Heading no. 5</h5>
        <p>This is the fifth heading of the page </p>
        <h6>This is heading no. 6</h6>
        <p>This is the sixth heading of the page</p>
</body>

</html>
```
